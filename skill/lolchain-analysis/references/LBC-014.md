## Analysis procedure

### Collect

Research algorithm, identifier-generation inputs, time/seed parameters and a preserved demonstration trace.

### Inspect

Reproduce candidate identifiers offline from the supplied algorithm and recorded inputs; compare with the trace.

### Correlate

Explain which generated identifiers were actually requested and what the demonstration returned.

### Interpretation limits

Keep this as research unless independent operational evidence exists. Do not probe generated identifiers on public networks by default.

## Catalog context

The Hydras paper experimentally studies IPFS-based bot management and identifier generation. This entry is a research demonstration, not an attributed campaign.

Examples: Hydras / Resource Identifier Generation Algorithms

### Detection guidance

Consider repeated content retrieval attempts and unknown IPFS clients; validate against legitimate distributed applications.

### Defensive approach

Retain CID and peer telemetry; apply policy to unsanctioned distributed-storage clients.
