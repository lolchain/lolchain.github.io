## Catalog context

The cited paper demonstrates a ransomware-as-a-service architecture using Ethereum and IPFS. It is a research design, not a documented operational group.

Examples: Smart-contract and IPFS RaaS research

### Detection guidance

Monitor unsanctioned chain and storage clients; do not equate normal Ethereum or IPFS use with ransomware.

### Defensive approach

Apply egress policy and endpoint prevention; use campaign-specific evidence for blocking decisions.
