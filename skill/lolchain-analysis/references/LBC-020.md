## Catalog context

ABC-Channel implements and evaluates a covert channel on Bitcoin testnet. This demonstrates a communication primitive; it does not establish widespread malware exfiltration.

Examples: ABC-Channel prototype

### Detection guidance

Consider unusual transaction creation and unauthorized blockchain clients; network metadata alone may not reveal covert contents.

### Defensive approach

Restrict unapproved transaction-capable software and retain relevant endpoint and network evidence.
