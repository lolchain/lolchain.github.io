### Collect

### Inspect

Inspect complete name_show/name_history results where available; preserve TTL/expiration and field interpretation.

### Correlate

### Interpretation limits

Do not treat all names in a namespace as malicious or infer owner identity from a shared gateway.

Methods: [Canisters, naming and NFTs](services.md).

## Catalog context

Mandiant documents malicious use of Emercoin namespaces such as .bazar and .coin, including gateway-based access.

Examples: GandCrab; Neutrino; criminal marketplaces

### Detection guidance

Monitor alternative namespaces and unapproved resolver or gateway use. A matching suffix alone is not proof of malware.

### Defensive approach

Apply DNS and egress policy using organizational need; investigate the resolved destination and process.

## ATT&CK assessment

Enterprise · Tactic only · Reported behavior

Alternative names support access to malicious infrastructure. A .bazar or .coin name by itself does not demonstrate a particular dynamic-resolution or DNS-tunneling technique.

- [TA0011 · Command and Control](https://attack.mitre.org/tactics/TA0011/)
